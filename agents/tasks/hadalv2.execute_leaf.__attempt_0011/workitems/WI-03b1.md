# WI-03b1 — Tier 2: CreatureDefs + simulation interactions (incl. friendly)

## Goal

Implement the 4-6 small/medium useful/neutral organisms the private roster
selects for tier 2 (section 11.1 distribution) as `CreatureDef` data plus
their simulation-side interactions, including the at-least-2 genuinely
helpful or mutually beneficial species (section 21) and the section 11.1
minimums the private roster assigns to this tier. Their signature rules and
friendly interactions are new gameplay behavior in the headless simulation,
evidenced by one headless signature-rule test per species plus the 2+
friendly floor check.

## Deliverables (checkable)

- One `CreatureDef` per selected tier-2 organism in
  `src/content/secret/hiddenCreatures.ts`.
- Simulation behavior for the interactions the private rubric assigns, from
  the section 21 candidate categories (feeding trade, guide behavior,
  warning behavior, shelter territory, investigating resource nodes,
  following light, response to repeated nonviolent interaction) - only the
  ones the private roster chose.
- At least one friendly interaction gives the player a practical advantage
  they can discover organically (section 21); no "press E to befriend"
  questification, no dialogue.
- Section 11.1 minimums assigned to this tier by the private roster (e.g.
  the looks-dangerous-but-safe organism, the apparently-harmless organism
  with a surprising second behavior, the wreck-incorporating organism) are
  implemented in simulation and tested here: the dangerous-looking-safe one
  must be safe in simulation, the second-behavior one must have both
  behaviors tested.

## Tests (node, real simulation, no mocks of the rules)

- One headless scenario per species for its signature rule, through the
  scenario harness (`src/sim/scenario.ts`).
- Friendly floor check (acceptance evidence for the "at least 2 creatures
  are beneficial" half of AC-roster-behavior, owned here): at least 2
  species deliver a mechanically beneficial interaction headlessly (e.g.
  feeding trade yields a resource; shelter territory blocks or deters a
  threat), through the production simulation.
- The apparent-harmless second behavior triggers under the designed
  condition and not before.
- The dangerous-looking-safe organism never harms or threatens the player
  in simulation.
- Spoiler containment for this item's artifacts: no creature name or secret
  description appears outside debug internals and the private content files
  (the tier's AC-roster-tests half; the roster-wide audit finalizes in
  WI-03d).

## Browser spot-check

None in this item - the tier's one browser spot-check (WI-03b2) covers
presentation; all evidence here is headless (section 70 layers). The
scenarios must still use the production simulation and world data, not
mocks of the rules.

## Constraints, assumptions, non-goals

- No spawns in production world data and no renderer work in this item
  (WI-03b2); no predators (WI-03c), no large/colossal staging (WI-03d). No
  new renderer architecture.
- Assumption: friendly interactions fit the existing resource/interaction
  rules in `src/sim/Simulation.ts`; if one needs a new meter or rule, add
  it minimally in the simulation per section 30 and say so in the evidence.
  Falsified if a friendly behavior requires a UI quest element - redesign
  privately per section 21 instead.
- Section 46 friendly-species test applies: each friendly species must be
  mechanically useful or emotionally memorable without speech or mascot
  status.
- Spoiler rules (sections 0, 12, 68): internal ids only in identifiers,
  tests, and commit messages ("implemented the mid-depth useful fauna tier"
  style).
